# Belgrade emblem

Source: [Small Coat of Arms Belgrade.svg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Small_Coat_of_Arms_Belgrade.svg), SVG by Matija, based on Đorđe Andrejević-Kun’s 1931 design.

Licence checked through the Wikimedia Commons imageinfo API on 17 October 2026: `LicenseShortName: Public domain`, `License: pd`, `Copyrighted: False`, `AttributionRequired: false`. Commons categorises the work as `PD Serbia official` (official symbols are outside copyright under Serbian law). The original API response is recorded in `commons-emblem.json`.

`arms-mono.svg` adapts the original paths to one ink: the fortress and ship print; the field and dark details are transparent knockouts. The enclosing shield remains outlined. The page uses this SVG as a CSS mask over currentColor. The source and adaptation are credited at the foot of the page.

Photos: Filip Biljić. Captions remain empty in photos.json.

# Map

The rivers (the Sava, the Danube and Lake Sava) are drawn from OpenStreetMap data, © OpenStreetMap contributors, under the [Open Database Licence](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/) ([copyright and licence](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright)). The water was downloaded on 17 October 2026 through the Overpass API (`natural=water` with `water=river|lake|reservoir`, and `waterway=riverbank`; box 44.75–44.855 N, 20.35–20.53 E), projected and simplified to SVG paths. The places come from OSM through Nominatim: Slavija (by way 1163791174), Beogradski sajam (way 180172873), Ada Ciganlija (relation 14675466). The credit is at the foot of the page.
