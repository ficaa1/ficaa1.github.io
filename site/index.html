About me

I am a cloud DevOps engineer at pass Culture, where I work on Google Cloud: GKE, Terraform, Helm, Argo, Argo workflows which inspired micko. Before that I spent two years at Intersec keeping national public warning systems running on Kubernetes, the kind that sends an alert to every phone in a country, and before that I looked after more than fifty Kubernetes clusters at Société Générale with Devoteam.

I studied distributed systems at Sorbonne Université and passed the Certified Kubernetes Administrator exam in 2022. French and Serbian are my native languages but I mostly write in English.

In 2023 I built a server out of my old PC parts. It's on proxmox and a bit of a mess.

The blog is where I write down how I did things, mainly so I can find them again. Photos and music are the things i sometimes wish i could be doing full time.
