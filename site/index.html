About me

I am a cloud DevOps engineer at pass Culture, where I work on Google Cloud: GKE, Terraform, Helm, Argo, and the IAM that holds it together. Before that I spent two years at Intersec keeping national public warning systems running on Kubernetes, the kind that sends an alert to every phone in a country, and before that I looked after more than fifty Kubernetes clusters at Société Générale with Devoteam.

I studied distributed systems at Sorbonne Université and passed the Certified Kubernetes Administrator exam in 2022. French and Serbian are my native languages; I write here in English.

In 2023 I built a server for my flat. It did not boot until I moved the RAM to different slots. It now runs Proxmox, a TrueNAS storage pool, a WireGuard gateway and a media stack that files things away by itself.

The blog is where I write down how I did things, mainly so I can find them again. The CV and the photos are the only pages here with a real stylesheet. Photos and music are the things I like that are not servers.
